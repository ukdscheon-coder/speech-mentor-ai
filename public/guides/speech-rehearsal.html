How to rehearse a speech with a teleprompter

Run through your talk three times before your next presentation, pitch or speech. Give each pass a different job.

1. Mark your pauses

Paste your script into Speech Mentor AI or choose a sample. Read the opening aloud and mark a pause after the first idea you want the audience to remember. Break long lines where you would naturally breathe.

2. Check your pace

Practise with the teleprompter at a comfortable speed. If you rush to keep up or look down for the next line, slow the scroll and repeat the same section. Time a full pass once you can deliver the opening comfortably.

3. Rehearse the transitions

Add a cue where you plan to change slides, point to a chart or look up. Run the talk again using those cues. Then practise answering one question your audience is likely to ask.
